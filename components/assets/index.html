About LNCT Group

Empowering Education. Fueling Innovation. Creating Impact.

Leading Educational Excellence

LNCT Group of Institutions & Companies was established in 1995 under the U.G. Rashtriya Education Trust by visionary Sri Jai Narain Chouksey. Over the past three decades, it has evolved into a leading educational and industrial conglomerate in Central India.

With 30+ institutions and business verticals across Engineering, Medical, Management, Pharmacy, Law, Nursing, and Research, LNCT has educated over 100,000 students and built an alumni network of 50,000+ professionals.

Our institutions are recognized for NIRF rankings, ISO 9001:2008 certification, and numerous national-level awards. We host world-class infrastructure, AICTE IDEA labs, innovation hubs, and India's most successful campus placements with offers reaching ₹1 Cr.
